How to use the room editor

  1. Open image… loads your room picture (or try the Sample room).
  2. Wand: click a coloured area, then Create from selection (Enter). Pick the kind first: a walkable area also turns holes in it (a table) into obstacles. Shift adds to the selection, Alt subtracts, Ctrl selects that colour everywhere.
  3. Polygon: click the corners, double-click or press Enter to finish, Esc cancels.
  4. Select: click a shape; drag a corner to move it, drag the inside to move the whole shape, Alt-click a corner to delete it, double-click an edge to add a corner, Delete removes the shape.
  5. Start: click to place a start position (name it first). The hero appears there.
  6. Walk-to: select a hotspot, then click where the hero should stand to use it.
  7. Name things in the right-hand panel. Export… gives the room JSON; Preview opens it in the real engine.

Scroll to zoom, drag with the middle or right mouse button (or hold Space) to pan. Keys: V P W S T tools, F fit, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo. Your work is saved in this browser automatically.
