Settings and help

Pick up the stick and the string, combine them (tap one in the bar, then the other), and fish the key out of the grate with the pole. Talk to the guard (?adapter=markdown, twine or ink picks the dialogue format). The trapdoor on the left leads to a cellar with its own music. The game opens with a short cutscene (Skip button or Esc). Mouse: click to act, right-click or hold to look. Touch: tap once to highlight, again to act, hold to look. D toggles debug.
