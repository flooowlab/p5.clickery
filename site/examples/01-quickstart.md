Click the floor to walk. Click the sign to use it, and right-click (or press and hold on a touch screen) to look at it.

The whole game is one small HTML file: a room described as data, the words as text keys, and two handlers for the sign. It is the same game as the first lessons in [Learn](../../learn/).
