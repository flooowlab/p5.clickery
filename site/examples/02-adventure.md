The game opens with a short cutscene (press the Skip button or Escape). Mouse: click to act, right-click or hold to look. Touch: tap once to highlight, again to act, hold to look. **D** shows the hotspot and floor outlines.

The guard's conversation is written in three formats that all behave the same. Choose which one you play:

- [Markdown](?) (the default)
- [Twine](?adapter=twine)
- [Ink](?adapter=ink)
